## TERMINOLOGY SHORTCUTS

### Physics/Math Concepts
- **Takens embedding** = time-delay reconstruction of phase portrait from 1D signal
- **Lyapunov exponent** = rate of trajectory divergence; λ > 0 = chaos
- **Bifurcation** = qualitative change in attractor structure at parameter threshold
- **Ricci flow** = geometric heat equation for metric: ∂g/∂t = −2 Ric
- **Fertility Test** = "Does new formulation open engineering lever old one doesn't?"
- **0-Truth protocol** = strip to bare measurements, prevent narrative inflation

### Biochemistry Concepts
- **KIE** (kinetic isotope effect) = ratio of reaction rates, H vs. D; temp-independent → quantum tunneling
- **Promoting vibrations** = protein scaffold oscillations that lower E_a
- **Chemiosmotic** = energy transduction via membrane gradient (Mitchell, 1960s)
- **ETc** = electron transport chain; redox gradient drives ATP synthesis
- **ΔG** = Gibbs free energy; = ΔH − TΔS; *system* property, not molecular
- **Wobble position** = 3rd codon letter; often irrelevant due to degeneracy

### Biology Concepts
- **Planckkon** = minimal mass unit (toy model of black hole microstructure)
- **White hole** = time-reversed black hole; S < 0; exponent suppressed
- **Degeneracy** = multiple microstates realize same macrostate (redundancy → robustness)
- **Basal metabolic rate** = "cosmological constant" of the cell; minimal dissipation for existence
- **Leuconostoc H32-02** = lactic acid bacterium; subject of surgical metabolic engineering (Potoki chat finale)
