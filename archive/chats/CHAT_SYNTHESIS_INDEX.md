### INFORMATION & ENTROPY
- **Entropy as correlations** → Not "disorder," but "accessible microstates"
  - S = k ln(W) — Boltzmann's definition
  - W = number of ways to realize observable macrostate
  - **Implication**: One planckcon (W=1) has S=0; two planckcons (W=2, can be swapped) have S=k·ln(2); N planckcons with interactions S = N(N−1)/2 (pairwise correlations)
  - **In biochemistry**: ΔG depends on W (number of molecular configurations available in context)

- **Redundancy as protection** → Degeneracy ≠ waste
  - Genetic code: 64 codons → 20 amino acids (4× redundancy)
  - Wobble position: 3rd letter often silent
  - **Information-theoretic stability**: Small errors don't propagate
  - **Physical analogy**: Topological protection in condensed matter

- **Information loss at boundaries** → Entropy production from interaction
  - Black hole: information crosses horizon, lost to exterior observer (S_BH > 0)
  - Membrane: molecules cross, context lost to other side (ΔG used)
  - **Unifying principle**: Boundary = irreversibility + entropy production

- **White hole paradox** → Negative entropy (time-reversed)
  - S = −N(N−1)/2 for white hole (Volovik)
  - Exponent suppressed: w ∝ exp(+S) = exp(−|S|)
  - **In biology**: Anabolism ΔG > 0, always coupled to ATP hydrolysis (ΔG_total < 0)
  - **Unresolved**: Cell maintains negative entropy continuously. Is it rare fluctuation or dissipative attractor?
