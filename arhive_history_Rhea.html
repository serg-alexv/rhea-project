What the spider does

You throw a claim into the web.
Rhea sends it to 1 independent model — they don't see each other.
Agreement = signal. Divergence = where the lie hides.
Every kill is stored in Aletheia — provenance chains, not marketing copy.

Endpoints

GET  /aletheia/search?q=   search the web
GET  /aletheia/stats       what's caught
GET  /aletheia/proofs      all kills
GET  /agents/status        who's hunting
POST /tribunal             verify a claim
POST /tribunal/ice         deep verification
GET  /health               pulse
