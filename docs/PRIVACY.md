## 2. Our Privacy Stance

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the platform. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We are not an advertising business. We are scientists.

---

## 3. Data We Collect

### 3a. If You Use the Platform Without an Account
- No personally identifiable information is collected
- Query history is stored **locally in your browser** (localStorage or similar)
- No tracking cookies are set (see Cookie Policy)
- Server logs may capture anonymized request metadata (timestamp, response code) for stability monitoring — these are not linked to individuals

### 3b. If You Create an Account (when available)
- **Email address**: used only for authentication and critical service notices
- **Query history**: stored server-side to enable session continuity and model routing — you can delete this at any time
- **No password stored in plaintext**: credentials are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt/Argon2)

### 3c. Data We Do Not Collect
- Real name, physical address, phone number
- Payment information (Rhea is free; no billing)
- Behavioral profiles, interests, or advertising identifiers
- Device fingerprints or persistent cross-site tracking identifiers

---

## 4. How We Use Data

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Email (if signup) | Authentication, critical notices | Until account deletion |
| Query history (server) | Session continuity, model routing | Until user deletes or account closed |
| Anonymized server logs | Stability, error diagnosis | 30 days rolling |

We do not use your data to train AI models. Queries may be forwarded to third-party AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.) per their respective privacy terms — review those independently.

---

## 5. Third-Party AI Providers

Rhea routes queries to external AI APIs. When you submit a query, it is transmitted to the relevant provider. Each provider has its own privacy policy:

- OpenAI: platform.openai.com/privacy
- Anthropic: anthropic.com/privacy
- Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy
- OpenRouter: openrouter.ai/privacy

We recommend reviewing these. We do not control how these providers handle API inputs.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties except:
- **Legal obligation**: if required by law, court order, or to protect safety
- **Service providers**: infrastructure providers who process data strictly to run the platform (under data processing agreements)
- **Open-source contributions**: if you contribute code, your GitHub username is public per GitHub's terms

---

## 7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect stored data:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
- Hashed credentials; no plaintext passwords
- Restricted access to production systems
- Regular security reviews (see SECURITY.md)

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery, in compliance with GDPR Article 33.

## 8. Your Rights

### GDPR (EU/UK users)
You have the right to:
- **Access** data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** processing
- **Data portability** (export your data in machine-readable format)
- **Object** to processing
- Lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority

### CCPA (California users)
You have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected and why
- Request deletion of personal information
- Opt out of sale of personal information (we do not sell — this right is automatically satisfied)
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

**To exercise any right**: email **celestica201@gmail.com** with subject "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

Rhea is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data, contact us for immediate removal.

- Local query history: you control this directly in your browser
- Server logs: 30-day rolling deletion
- Deleted account data: purged within 30 days of deletion request
