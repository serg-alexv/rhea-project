# Attention/Behavior Source Collection (Scientist-Written Filter)

Date: 2026-02-27
Owner: ORION

## Classification rule
A source is marked **Scientist-written** when it is primarily authored by researchers and published as:
- peer-reviewed journal/conference work, or
- a scientific monograph/book chapter, or
- an academic research lab publication/preprint.

A source is marked **Non-scientific / practice-policy** when it is primarily:
- UX industry guidance,
- regulator/legal guidance,
- policy text.

## A) Scientist-written sources
1. Michael Posner (1980), *Orienting of attention* — Journal of Experimental Psychology.  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7367577/
2. Steven Yantis, Jonides (1984), abrupt visual onsets and selective attention — Journal of Experimental Psychology.  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6238122/
3. Friesen & Kingstone / related gaze-cueing work — reflexive orienting from eye gaze.  
   Links: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11273424/ , https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15629217/
4. Simons & Chabris (1999), inattentional blindness ("gorilla" experiment) — Perception.  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10694957/
5. Rensink et al. / reviews on change blindness (scientific literature).  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21223921/
6. Treisman & Gelade (1980), Feature Integration Theory — Cognitive Psychology.  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7351125/
7. Kahneman & Tversky (1979), *Prospect Theory* — Econometrica.  
   Link: https://www.econometricsociety.org/publications/econometrica/browse/1979/03/01/prospect-theory-analysis-decision-under-risk
8. Johnson & Goldstein (defaults effect) — Science / behavioral decision research.  
   Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15614141/
9. Samuelson & Zeckhauser (1988), status quo bias — Journal of Risk and Uncertainty.  
   Link: https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:kap:jrisku:v:1:y:1988:i:1:p:7-59
10. Princeton Web Transparency project, dark patterns at scale — academic CS research.
    Link: https://webtransparency.cs.princeton.edu/dark-patterns/
