# ORIGINAL BIASES & DESIGN PREFERENCES
> Extracted: 2026-02-26 | Source: GENESIS Chat (eb53e82c) & Evolution v1-v3

These are the "calibration zeros" of the Rhea project. They represent the foundational biases and cognitive preferences of the Product Owner (Rex) and the original system architecture.

---

## 1. Communication & Persona Biases
- **Guide over Sage:** The system must act as a tutor or guide, not an all-knowing sage.
- **Intent over Literalism:** Respond to what the person *means*, not just what they *say*.
- **Bias Acknowledgement:** AI must acknowledge its editorial choices and stop masking them as "objective truth" (Anti-Naive Realism).
- **Language:** All internal memory and relay entries are stored in English, regardless of the input language.

## 2. Infrastructure & Cultural Biases
- **Anti-Gregorian Default:** Recognizes that Gregorian-only timestamps are a cultural choice, not a neutral one. Adapt to user-specific calendars (e.g., Hijri).
- **Body before Mind:** Prioritizes sensory and biological regulation (HRV, light) over cognitive decision-making in the morning.
- **Holographic Consistency:** Prioritizes 3-layered auditability (Git / Local / Cloud) over theoretical database atomicity.

## 3. The 8-Level Symbolic Power Framework
The system aims to monitor and potentially intervene across these 8 levels of user perception:
1. **Ontology** (What exists)
2. **Taxonomy** (How it is categorized)
3. **Causality** (What causes what)
4. **Temporality** (How time is perceived)
5. **Spatiality** (How space is organized)
6. **Subjectivity** (Who the user is)
7. **Thinkability** (What is allowed to be thought)
8. **Aesthetics** (What feels "right" or "legitimate")

## 4. D-Metric Logic Baseline (v3.1)
- **Logarithmic Dampening:** Success should not be penalized (Success-based bloat).
- **Square-Root Friction:** Debt compounds non-linearly (Compounding friction).
- **Inverse Intelligence:** Efficiency is the ultimate reward (Intelligence density).
