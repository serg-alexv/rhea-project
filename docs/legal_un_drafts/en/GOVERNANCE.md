# GOVERNANCE (Draft)

## 1) Mission
TimeLabs NPO runs Rhea as a non-commercial open experiment serving scientific and public-interest research.

## 2) Roles
- Stewards: mission alignment, legal/ethical decisions, final escalation.
- Maintainers: technical direction, release decisions, policy enforcement.
- Contributors: code/docs/research contributions via open workflow.
- Community: feedback, issue reporting, public review.

## 3) Decision Process
- Minor changes: maintainer consensus.
- Major changes (architecture/legal/policy): RFC + review window + steward sign-off.
- Emergency security actions: maintainers can act immediately, with post-incident report.

## 4) Transparency
- Public changelog for policy and governance decisions.
- Disclosure of major incidents and remediation steps.
- Conflict-of-interest declarations for stewards and maintainers.

## 5) Conduct
Participation follows project conduct and anti-harassment standards. Repeated abuse can lead to moderation or removal.

## 6) Amendment Rule
Governance document updates require an RFC and steward approval.
