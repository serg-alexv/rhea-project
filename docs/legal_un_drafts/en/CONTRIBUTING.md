# CONTRIBUTING (Draft)

## 1) Scope
Contributions are welcome for code, docs, tests, UI, research notes, and operational tooling.

## 2) Workflow
- Fork/branch from default branch.
- Keep changes focused.
- Add or update tests when behavior changes.
- Open PR with clear summary and risk notes.

## 3) Sign-off (Required)
All commits must include Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off:

`Signed-off-by: Full Name <email@example.com>`

## 4) Licensing of Contributions
By contributing, you agree your contribution is licensed under the repository/project license for that path, and that you have rights to submit it.

## 5) Security
Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before coordinated fix. Use the project security channel.

## 6) Quality Gates
PRs may be blocked on failing tests, failing lint, failing license checks, or missing provenance.
