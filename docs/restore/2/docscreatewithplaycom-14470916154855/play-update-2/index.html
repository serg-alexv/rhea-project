We're working on something new.

We want to express our sincere thanks for your support of Play — it has been an incredible journey. Starting April 20, 2026, we will no longer support the Play iOS and macOS apps.

Prorated refunds will be issued to users on a paid plan. To help with the transition, all users will have access to the Play to Xcode feature.

Please reference our FAQs for additional details. If you have questions or need help, our team is here for you at support@createwithplay.com.

Thank you again from everyone on the Play team.
