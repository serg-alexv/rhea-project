Design, prototype, & ship with the power of Play AI.

Play AI is built into the design canvas — helping you generate ideas, add effects, and prototype interactions without leaving your flow.

Don't be limited by your traditional design tools.

Design in Play or import from Figma.

Design on a freeform canvas with access to real native elements like maps, pickers, and input text fields.

Tired of prototypes that don't feel real?

Interactions in Play are performant, interruptible, and use Apple's Core Animation so your prototypes use the same tech as a finished mobile app.

Frictionless sharing with Apple App Clips.

No web simulations. No app installs. It's the easiest, fastest, highest quality way to share prototypes.

Export your project in Play to Xcode.

Export styles, components, full pages & flows directly to Xcode. Integrate external data and keep your Play and Xcode files in sync.
