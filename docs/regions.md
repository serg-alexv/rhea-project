## 4) Hofstede-based grouping

- No single canonical fixed list.
- Countries are grouped by proximity across dimensions:
  - Individualism
  - Power distance
  - Uncertainty avoidance
  - Masculinity/femininity
  - Long-term orientation
  - Indulgence/restraint
